02 · Product Platform · Mosaic CMS

Turning an internal CMS into a launch-speed advantage

A platform and workflow redesign for no-developer page releases.

14 → 1.4 days90% faster launchesInternal NPS 40.8

Product challenge

Template launches were slow, and a fragmented widget system increased operational and development complexity.

Evidence

The existing system spanned 20 widget types and 50 configurations, while internal workflow feedback exposed usability friction.

Decision & trade-off

Standardised the system into three DLS-compliant universal widgets covering 36 configurations—trading unnecessary variation for reuse, governance, and speed.

My contribution

Led the CMS UX and workflow overhaul, aligned requirements with Design and Engineering, and drove validation through UAT and internal feedback.

Outcome

Reduced template launch time by 90%, from 14 days to 1.4 days, while raising internal NPS to 40.8.

What this demonstrates

Platform thinking, system simplification, internal-product empathy, SDUI, and operational leverage.

Creating repeat value after the car transaction

+41% MAU+15% initial retention3 core journeys

Product challenge

Product scope

What this demonstrates
